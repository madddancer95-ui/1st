Five Things to Talk Through

These are conversation prompts, not pain ratings. Keep them handy when you speak with your artist.

01

Placement, Size and Detail

Show the artist your references, preferred placement and approximate width and height. Ask whether the design needs more space or a different approach at that placement.

Ask: Can we compare size and placement options before I decide?

02

The Session Plan

Ask what the appointment will involve, how much time to set aside and whether the project may need more than one session.

Ask: What happens from the design discussion to the end of the appointment?

03

Breaks and Communication

Discuss breaks before the session begins. Agree how to ask the artist to pause and how you will let them know if you want to stop or reconsider.

Ask: How should I ask for a break, and how will we decide when to continue?

04

Your Questions and Boundaries

Tell the artist which part of the appointment concerns you. Ask for an explanation of anything unfamiliar before agreeing to the design and session plan.

Ask: Could you talk me through the part I am unsure about?

05

The Next Step

Ask the studio to confirm your design discussion, quote and appointment details before you book.

Ask: What do I need to confirm before choosing an appointment?
